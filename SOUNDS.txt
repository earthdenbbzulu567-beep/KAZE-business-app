KAZE sound cues
===============

Put the files in static/sounds/ (already in this pack).

Cues
- sale_complete   recorded / auto sale
- sale_completed  till ticket rung up
- stock_update    stock add / edit / import / reorder
- notification    notifications list
- reminder        reminders with overdue work
- low_stock       stock or dashboard when items are below the threshold
- success         other successful saves
- error           failed actions

Bell in the top bar mutes or unmutes. Settings → Appearance → Sound cues
does the same on this device. Preview each cue from Settings.

First browser play may wait for a click (browser autoplay rules). After
that, flashes and page cues play on their own.
