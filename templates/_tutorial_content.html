1

Dashboard

Your home base. See total income, expenses, and profit at a glance, track trends on the chart, and quickly add new income or expense entries. If you've set a monthly revenue goal in Settings, you'll see your progress toward it here too.

2

Stock / Inventory

Add products with a cost price and selling price. The app tracks quantity automatically as you record sales, and warns you when something drops below your low-stock threshold. Use "Check Low Stock Now" any time to get an on-demand email report.

3

Sales

Record a sale against an item in your stock — it automatically calculates profit and reduces your inventory. Attach a sale to a saved customer, or just type a name for a walk-in. Every sale can be downloaded as a PDF receipt.

4

Customers

Keep a proper contact list with email, phone, and notes. Each customer's page shows their lifetime order count and total spend, calculated from their sales history.

5

Documents

Create invoices, contracts, and reports. Every document can be downloaded as a polished PDF, with your business name, currency, and tax rate applied automatically from Settings.

6

Book keeping

Cash ledger plus quotations, invoices, delivery notes, GRNs, receipts, vouchers, cheques, bank slips, and payroll papers. Each one can be saved and downloaded as a PDF.

7

SWOT Analysis

Step back and think strategically: what are your business's Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats? Save as many analyses as you like over time to track how your thinking evolves, and export any of them as a PDF.

8

Business Plan

A single living document for your mission, target market, marketing strategy, operations, financial plan, and goals. Update it whenever your strategy shifts, and export it as a PDF whenever you need to share it (with a bank, a partner, or yourself in six months).

9

Recurring Items, Purchase Orders & Budgets

Schedule recurring expenses or invoices so they generate themselves. Track stock on order from suppliers and receive it straight into inventory. Set a monthly spending limit per category and watch it in real time.

10

Settings

Set your business name, currency, and tax rate (used on PDFs), your low-stock threshold and notification email, your monthly revenue goal, and appearance preferences like theme and font.

⇅

Importing & exporting your data

Look for the "⬇️ Export / Import" button on Stock, Customers, Cash Book, and the Dashboard — it opens a small popup where you can download a CSV backup or upload one to bulk-add records.

11

Tasks, Notes & Calendar

Use Tasks for jobs with due dates, Notes as a scratch pad, and Calendar to see upcoming recurring items, purchase orders, and task due dates in one list.

12

Calculator, backup & reorder

The profit calculator estimates margin before you sell. Settings can change your password and download or restore a JSON backup. On Stock, "Reorder low stock" drafts purchase orders for items under your threshold.

13

Book keeping documents

Open Book keeping in the sidebar. Pick a document type such as Quotation, Sales Invoice, GRN, Payslip, or Cheque, fill it in, and download the PDF.

14

Account tracking

Journals (purchases, sales, returns, general, petty cash), ledgers (general, debtors, creditors), plus Trading Account, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow. Sales and purchases post themselves; you can type extra journal lines.

15

Reminders, tax, statements, repeat sale, price list

Reminders lists overdue tasks and late orders. Tax summary estimates VAT from your rate. Each customer has a Statement PDF. Sales has Repeat. Stock has a printable price list.
